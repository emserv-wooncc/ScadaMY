Automation for everyone

From home projects to industrial environment, ScadaMY is the ideal solution. With more than 20 communication protocols, alarm management and support for creation of reports and HMIs, ScadaMY provides an accessible and reliable platform for automation.

ScadaMY Project website

Have freedom

ScadaMY is free and open-source software. Licensed under GNU GPL v3, ScadaMY is a SCADA system without any restrictions on usage time or quantity of Tags / Data Points. Using ScadaMY, your creativity is the only limit.

Need help?

The ScadaMY Forum is the place where the ScadaMY community shares tips, ideas and answers questions. Are you a company or need special paid support? In the ScadaMY Forum you can also have access to ScadaMY 'integrators', people and companies that provide commercial solutions involving ScadaMY.

ScadaMY Forum website

The future of the project

In 2016, a group of developers from outside Brazil started the Scada-LTS project, a ScadaMY fork with new features and the latest technologies. In the future, the current versions of ScadaMY, based on Mango M2M, will be replaced by new versions based on Scada-LTS.

If you are a ScadaMY developer or enthusiast, please consider contributing to Scada-LTS project.
